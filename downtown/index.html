The latest in Downtown

November 12, 2026

Ashwood Park, at 10875 NE 12th Street, is a 2.84-acre park with an accessible multi-use field. Field rentals go through the city at 425-452-6914. Source

November 5, 2026

Bellevue Downtown Park, at 10201 NE 4th St, has a 240-foot waterfall into a reflecting pond, a water spray playground in summer, formal gardens, public art, public Wi-Fi and a 10-acre lawn. Source

October 29, 2026

The Downtown code rewrite was on Wednesday's Planning Commission agenda. The draft widens sidewalks from 12 to 14 feet on Main Street, NE 10th, NE 12th and parts of 112th Avenue NE, requires benches and trash cans every 150 feet, and cuts required ground-floor shops from 100% to 75% of the frontage on main streets. A hearing is expected by early 2027. Source

October 22, 2026

Downtown has required visitor parking in new residential buildings since 2017, and the parking rewrite asks whether to extend it citywide. Staff recommend one stall per 20 units up to 100 units, then one per 40. The city's curb counts found many Downtown blocks completely full. Source

October 15, 2026

The city has opened comment on Downtown Livability 2.0, the first broad rewrite of the Downtown land use code since 2017. It covers Main Street in Old Bellevue, affordable housing and commercial space, and the Grand Connection. A full strike draft is posted, and comments are taken until council adopts it: file 26-120379-AD, planner Arvind Gopalakrishnan, AGopalak@bellevuewa.gov. Source

October 8, 2026

Schnitzer West's long-pending Arcadian design review, at 120 106th Avenue NE (filed in 2022), will now be reviewed under the city's newer mixed-use housing code (Ordinance 6906). The city has not decided the application yet. File 22-113780-LD. The proposed budget also includes $3 million to run paid on-street parking Downtown and on some BelRed and Wilburton streets. Permit · Budget

October 1, 2026

The billion-dollar expansion of Bellevue Square is shelved. Kemper Development confirmed on September 28 that "The Bellevue" — three towers on 3.6 acres at the southeast corner of Bellevue Way NE and NE 4th, about 1.75 million square feet with 234 residential units, a 157-room hotel, nearly 300,000 square feet of retail and six levels of underground parking — will not advance under current ownership, and is left to whoever buys the Bellevue Collection. Existing properties keep operating and the 2026 event calendar stands. Source

September 24, 2026

Kemper Development has hired a broker to lead a sale of the Bellevue Collection, the Square-Place-Bellevue Collection block that anchors downtown retail. Nothing has changed on the ground yet; this is the start of a process. Source

September 17, 2026

Expect shifting bus bays at the Transit Center until January, and the west half closing in October. Council hears budget comment Tuesday at 6 p.m. in Room 1E-126; Experience Bellevue fills City Hall Saturday. Source

Questions people ask

Is this a real estate newsletter?

No. It is a Bellevue newsletter that happens to know the housing market. Three of the four sections are about living here. The fourth tells you what homes are selling for, because in Bellevue that is news too.

Who writes it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publisher. Every item comes from Bellevue's own public record (council agendas, permit notices, school district and transit updates, parks calendars) and links its source. It is researched and written with AI tools, and an editor reads every issue before it goes out.

How often, and is it really free?

Once a week, Thursday at 6 AM, about five minutes to read. Free, no paywall, unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Who owns it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publishing company. The newsletter is free, and no sponsor buys coverage. Our standards.

What happens with my address?

It is used for one thing: a monthly estimate of what your home would sell for today. It is shared with a local real estate brokerage under the same ownership as our publisher, which may contact you about your home, and is never sold or posted. Remove it any time by replying to any issue.
