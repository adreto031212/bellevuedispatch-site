The latest in Factoria

November 12, 2026

The King County Factoria Recycling & Transfer Station, at 13800 SE 32nd Street, is open weekdays 6:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. and weekends 8:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. Its household hazardous waste drop-off is open Tuesday to Friday, 8 to 4, and weekends, 9 to 5, and closed Thursday, Nov. 26, for the holiday. Hours · Drop-off

October 29, 2026

Sunset Park, at 2837 139th Avenue SE off SE Eastgate Way, has a basketball court, a playground, picnic tables, open lawn and nature trails. Source

October 22, 2026

The Eastrail segment now in for permits crosses I-90 just west of Factoria and connects at 32nd Street with the city's Mountains to Sound trail. King County puts construction at 2028–2031 and opening in 2031. Source

October 15, 2026

Sunset Ravine Trail, at 13400 SE 40th Street, is getting new surfacing, fencing, benches, signs and wayfinding posts, expected in early 2027. The city notes the work is affected by nearby state highway construction. Source

October 8, 2026

Council's consent agenda Tuesday included a three-year lease extension, through July 2029, for the police community substation at Factoria Mall. The city has had a substation there since 1997. Rent starts at $1,632 a month and rises 4% a year. On the ten-year list, improvements at the Factoria Boulevard and SE 38th Street intersection are funded in all three scenarios ($5.6 million). Lease · Scenarios

October 1, 2026

The Factoria neighborhood area plan had its final-review public hearing at the Planning Commission on September 23 and now goes to City Council on November 10, alongside Eastgate's. Both plans came out of about ten months of community input and set policy on neighborhood identity, centers, gathering space, mobility and environment. The draft is posted for reading before council takes it up. Source

September 24, 2026

Factoria's draft neighborhood area plan is in the same round as Eastgate's — the 2025–26 cycle of the Great Neighborhoods program. The city expects the Planning Commission to hold its hearing on the Factoria plan this month, with both plans completed this year and adopted by council after the commission's recommendation. Source

September 17, 2026

Boot Barn has taken an 11,169-square-foot space at 4026 Factoria Mall SE in The Marketplace at Factoria, per the center's directory; no opening date yet. It would be the chain's 12th Washington store. Source

Questions people ask

Is this a real estate newsletter?

No. It is a Bellevue newsletter that happens to know the housing market. Three of the four sections are about living here. The fourth tells you what homes are selling for, because in Bellevue that is news too.

Who writes it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publisher. Every item comes from Bellevue's own public record (council agendas, permit notices, school district and transit updates, parks calendars) and links its source. It is researched and written with AI tools, and an editor reads every issue before it goes out.

How often, and is it really free?

Once a week, Thursday at 6 AM, about five minutes to read. Free, no paywall, unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Who owns it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publishing company. The newsletter is free, and no sponsor buys coverage. Our standards.

What happens with my address?

It is used for one thing: a monthly estimate of what your home would sell for today. It goes to a local real estate brokerage under the same ownership as our publisher only if you tick the sharing box on the form; that brokerage may then contact you about your home. Leave the box empty and it stays with us. Never sold or posted. Remove it any time by replying to any issue.
