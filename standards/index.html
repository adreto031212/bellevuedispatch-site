Sponsors

Every paid placement carries the word "Sponsored". Ads never buy coverage. No real estate brokerages or agents and no political ads as sponsors.

Market numbers

Sold homes and market numbers come from the Northwest Multiple Listing Service and public records, with the source named.

Your information

Your signup, and your address if you add one, are shared with a local real estate brokerage owned by our publisher, which may contact you about your home. Nothing is sold, and advertisers never get reader emails. Unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Corrections

Tell us by replying to any issue or writing to hello@bellevuedispatch.com. Corrections run in the next issue and on the web copy within a day.
