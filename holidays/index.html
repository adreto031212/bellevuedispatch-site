Bellevue · Holiday guide 2026

Every Bellevue holiday date in one place.

Each one checked on the organizer's own page on Nov. 11. New dates, changes and the neighbors' lights list come in the Thursday issue.

Fri Nov 13 – Mon Jan 18 · Daily; hours vary by day

Bellevue Downtown Ice Rink

The outdoor seasonal rink in Downtown Park, run by the Bellevue Downtown Association, opens for its 2026-27 season.

Bellevue Downtown Park, 10201 NE 4th St · Paid admission, skate rental included; prices on the rink's ticket page · Organizer's page

Thu Nov 26 – Fri Dec 25 · Nov 26, Nov 27 and Dec 25

City of Bellevue offices closed

City offices close for Thanksgiving, the day after Thanksgiving and Christmas Day.

Bellevue City Hall and city offices · Organizer's page

Thu Nov 26 · All day

King County transfer stations closed on Thanksgiving

King County offices and its transfer stations and recycling facilities close on Thanksgiving Day; garbage pickup changes come from your own hauler.

King County solid waste facilities, including the Factoria Recycling and Transfer Station · Organizer's page

Thu Nov 26 – Fri Jan 1 · Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, New Year's Day

Sound Transit holiday schedules

Link and ST Express run Sunday schedules on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Day; on Nov 27 Link runs a Saturday schedule and ST Express a weekday one.

Link light rail and ST Express · Regular fares · Organizer's page

Fri Nov 27 – Thu Dec 24 · 7 PM nightly

Snowflake Lane

A nightly street parade of drummers, dancers and Santa with machine-made snow, now in its 22nd year.

Bellevue Way NE between NE 4th and NE 8th St (Bellevue Square to Lincoln Square) · Free; free parking at Bellevue Square, Lincoln Square and Bellevue Place · Organizer's page

Fri Nov 27 · After the 7 PM Snowflake Lane parade

Wintergarden Tree Lighting

Santa counts down to lighting a 30-foot tree, with photos, holiday music, cookies and cocoa.

Bellevue Place Wintergarden · Free · Organizer's page

Sat Nov 28 – Sun Jan 3 · 4:30 PM first entry; last entry 9 PM (8 PM on $5 nights)

Garden d'Lights

The botanical garden's paths are lit for evening walks through the holidays; closed Christmas Day.

Bellevue Botanical Garden, 12001 Main St · $10 ages 11+; $5 Mon-Thu Nov 30-Dec 3 and Dec 7-10; kids 10 and under free · Organizer's page

Sat Dec 5 · 5 to 6:30 PM

Holiday Barn Lighting at Kelsey Creek Farm

A countdown to switching on the farm's lights, carols, Santa, kids' crafts and the animals' dinner in the barn.

Kelsey Creek Farm, 410 130th Pl SE · Free parking, admission and most activities; some tickets sold on site · Organizer's page

Sun Dec 6 · 4:30 PM

Grand Menorah Lighting

Chabad of Downtown Bellevue lights the menorah on the third night of Chanukah, with hot drinks and songs.

510 Bellevue Way NE (the former Bellevue Arts Museum) · Free; reservations appreciated · Organizer's page

Sat Dec 12 · 8:40 to 9 PM

Christmas Ship at Meydenbauer Bay

The Argosy Christmas Ship stops offshore for about 20 minutes while its choir sings carols over a speaker for people on the beach.

Meydenbauer Bay · Free to watch from shore · Organizer's page
