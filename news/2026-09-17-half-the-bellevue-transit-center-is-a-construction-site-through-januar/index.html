Half the Bellevue Transit Center is a construction site through January

September 17, 2026 · Bellevue Dispatch, issue 1 · All Bellevue news

Sound Transit is rebuilding the bays for the coming Stride bus rapid transit lines: east half first, west half from October, so bay assignments keep moving. The fall service change also retired ST Express Routes 550 and 554, the old Seattle buses; 2 Line schedules are unchanged. Source · Service change
