Bellevue · One useful thing

clear the storm drains by the curb

November 5, 2026 · Bellevue Dispatch, issue 8 · All Bellevue news

Fall leaves can block the storm drains next to your curb. Clear only the drains next to the curb, not the ones out in the street, and do not walk or drive through flooded areas. If a drain stays clogged after raking, call Bellevue Utilities at 425-452-7840. Bellevue Utilities
