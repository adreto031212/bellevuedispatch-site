A county sales tax starts in January. The question is whether Bellevue keeps its share

October 15, 2026 · Bellevue Dispatch, issue 5 · All Bellevue news

King County passed a 0.01% tax for children and family services on October 6, one cent on $100. A city that adopts the same tax collects what is spent inside its limits instead, and the two do not stack. Bellevue's version, on Tuesday's agenda, would keep about $1 million a year here. Source
