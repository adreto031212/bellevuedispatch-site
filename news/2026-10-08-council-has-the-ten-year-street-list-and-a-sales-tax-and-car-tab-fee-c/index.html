Council has the ten-year street list, and a sales tax and car-tab fee could pay for more of it

October 8, 2026 · Bellevue Dispatch, issue 4 · All Bellevue news

At Tuesday's budget session, staff laid out three scenarios for 2027–2036 transportation projects. The first uses existing revenue ($294 million). The second adds a 0.1% sales tax through the Transportation Benefit District (about $11 million a year, $398 million in all). The third also adds a $20 vehicle fee, which raises another $2 million a year for neighborhood projects ($418 million in all). Next: October 13 (utility rates), a hearing on October 27, and adoption on November 17. Source
