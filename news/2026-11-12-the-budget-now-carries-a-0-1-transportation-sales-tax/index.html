The budget now carries a 0.1% transportation sales tax

November 12, 2026 · Bellevue Dispatch, issue 9 · All Bellevue news

On Nov. 3, council voted 5–1 for a hybrid option that uses the Transportation Benefit District for a 0.1%, 10-year sales tax, with no car-tab fee increase. Final deliberations were set for Nov. 10, and adoption is scheduled for Nov. 17. Council roundup · Budget schedule
