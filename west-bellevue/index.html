The latest in West Bellevue

November 12, 2026

Surrey Downs Park, at 11177 SE 4th Street, is 10.5 acres with a Great Lawn ringed by a path, a playground, basketball, first-come picnic shelters, a restroom and parking. Its berms look out toward Mount Rainier. Source

November 5, 2026

Enatai Beach Park, at 3519 108th Ave SE, has beach access with a swimming dock, a non-motorized watercraft launch, picnic tables, restrooms, playgrounds, fishing spots and a boat house. Source

October 29, 2026

Chism Beach Park, at 9600 SE 11th Street, is a Lake Washington beach park with a dock, a playground, walking paths and large picnic areas. It opens 30 minutes before sunrise and closes 30 minutes after sunset. Source

October 22, 2026

An owner at 1223 96th Avenue SE has applied to build a casita, replace stairs and add a personal tram on a steep slope. Comments by 5 p.m. October 28. Source

October 15, 2026

West Bellevue is next in line for a neighborhood plan rewrite: council has scheduled it, with Woodridge, for 2027–2028, once Lake Hills and West Lake Sammamish finish. Source

October 8, 2026

A rebuild of the Lake Washington Boulevard and NE 10th Street intersection is funded in all three scenarios ($7 million). An upgrade of the NE 8th Street and 92nd Avenue NE signal is funded only if the sales tax passes ($2 million). The Meydenbauer South Reservoir community meeting is tonight, 6:30 to 8 p.m., City Hall Room 1E-108. Source

October 1, 2026

The Eastrail is closing its I-90 gap through West Bellevue. The city has filed a land use application to build a sidewalk or paved shoulder on the south side of SE 32nd Street from 118th Avenue SE to the west side of the train trestle, plus a shared-use path on the west side of 118th Avenue SE from I-90 to SE 32nd Street — the Mountains to Sound Greenway connection. File 26-114712-LO; the comment period runs to 5 p.m. October 7. Source

September 24, 2026

A shoreline permit is open for comment at 1863 Killarney Way (file 26-107015-WG): repair of an existing dock with 761 square feet of grated decking, a new 238-square-foot ramp, removal of a 578-square-foot moorage cover, and pile work. The shoreline comment period runs to 5 p.m. October 9. Source

September 17, 2026

Meydenbauer Bay Park Phase 2 is in a roughly three-year design and permitting stretch, with construction now expected to begin in late 2029. The plan opens a direct Main Street and 100th Avenue NE gateway from downtown to the water. Source

Questions people ask

Is this a real estate newsletter?

No. It is a Bellevue newsletter that happens to know the housing market. Three of the four sections are about living here. The fourth tells you what homes are selling for, because in Bellevue that is news too.

Who writes it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publisher. Every item comes from Bellevue's own public record (council agendas, permit notices, school district and transit updates, parks calendars) and links its source. It is researched and written with AI tools, and an editor reads every issue before it goes out.

How often, and is it really free?

Once a week, Thursday at 6 AM, about five minutes to read. Free, no paywall, unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Who owns it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publishing company. The newsletter is free, and no sponsor buys coverage. Our standards.

What happens with my address?

It is used for one thing: a monthly estimate of what your home would sell for today. It is shared with a local real estate brokerage under the same ownership as our publisher, which may contact you about your home, and is never sold or posted. Remove it any time by replying to any issue.
