The latest in Northwest Bellevue

November 12, 2026

Clyde Beach Park, at 2 92nd Ave NE, is a 2.23-acre beach park with a boathouse, a swimming beach and shower, a launch for kayaks and other non-motorized boats, restrooms and two playgrounds. It is open from 30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset. Source

November 5, 2026

Meydenbauer Bay Park, at 9899 Lake Washington Blvd NE, has a beach house with restrooms and showers, a pedestrian pier, walking paths, picnic areas and a children's play area. It is open 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily. Source

October 29, 2026

Council approved the Bellewood Townhomes rezone on October 20. It covers about 3.2 acres at 2229 and 2233 112th Avenue NE and amends a zoning agreement from Ordinance 2300. Source

October 22, 2026

A three-story art gallery is proposed at 11217 NE 15th Street, on a quarter-acre office-zoned lot that now holds a single house. Comments by 5 p.m. October 28; a virtual design review meeting is November 9 at 6 p.m. The Bellewood Townhomes rezone on 112th Avenue NE was on Tuesday's agenda for final action. Source

October 15, 2026

Hidden Valley Park, at 1903 112th Avenue NE, covers 17.31 acres with three softball fields, a lighted tennis court, a sports court and two play areas. Its fieldhouse is run with the Boys & Girls Clubs of Bellevue. Source

October 8, 2026

The Hearing Examiner has recommended approving the Bellewood Townhomes rezone. It would lift a 1976 zoning agreement so the 1978 office buildings at 2229 and 2233 112th Avenue NE, near NE 24th Street, can be replaced with 42 townhomes. Council took it up Tuesday, and its final vote is tentatively set for October 20. Source

October 1, 2026

Today is the last regular Thursday market: the Bellevue Farmers Market runs 3 to 7 p.m. at 1717 Bellevue Way NE, then closes the season, with two Saturday fall markets on October 31 and November 21, 10 to 3. Meanwhile the 100th Avenue NE walkways project is in its second phase, with sidewalk, shared-use pathway and raised intersection work on NE 18th Street, 98th Avenue NE and NE 21st Street running through the fall. Market · Walkways

September 24, 2026

Two Thursdays left. The Bellevue Farmers Market runs 3 to 7 p.m. at 1717 Bellevue Way NE through October 1, then closes the regular season; two Saturday fall markets follow on October 31 and November 21, 10 to 3. Source

September 17, 2026

100th Avenue NE is closed between NE 13th and NE 17th through November for the $7.3 million walkways project: a shared-use path on 100th from NE 14th to NE 24th, new sidewalks on NE 18th, NE 21st and 98th, and raised crosswalks. Finish is expected this fall. Source

Questions people ask

Is this a real estate newsletter?

No. It is a Bellevue newsletter that happens to know the housing market. Three of the four sections are about living here. The fourth tells you what homes are selling for, because in Bellevue that is news too.

Who writes it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publisher. Every item comes from Bellevue's own public record (council agendas, permit notices, school district and transit updates, parks calendars) and links its source. It is researched and written with AI tools, and an editor reads every issue before it goes out.

How often, and is it really free?

Once a week, Thursday at 6 AM, about five minutes to read. Free, no paywall, unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Who owns it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publishing company. The newsletter is free, and no sponsor buys coverage. Our standards.

What happens with my address?

It is used for one thing: a monthly estimate of what your home would sell for today. It goes to a local real estate brokerage under the same ownership as our publisher only if you tick the sharing box on the form; that brokerage may then contact you about your home. Leave the box empty and it stays with us. Never sold or posted. Remove it any time by replying to any issue.
