The latest in Wilburton

November 12, 2026

The Bellevue Botanical Garden, at 12001 Main Street, has 53 acres of gardens, woodland and wetland, open daily dawn to dusk. Garden d'Lights runs Nov. 28 to Jan. 3 (closed Christmas Day), with timed tickets sold online only: $10 for ages 11 and up, $5 on Nov. 30–Dec. 3 and Dec. 7–10, and free for kids 10 and under. Source

November 5, 2026

Wilburton Hill Park, at 12400 Main Street, covers 105.49 acres, the city's largest upland park. It has a Lake to Lake Trail link, a zip-line play area, two ball fields and a soccer field. Source

October 29, 2026

Council approved the EverGlen Village rezone on October 20: 0.62 acre at 12500 NE 10th Place moves from office to midrise mixed-use residential. The Planning Commission takes up the next phase of the Wilburton Sustainable District November 18. Source

October 22, 2026

The EverGlen Village rezone, 0.62 acre at 12500 NE 10th Place from office to midrise mixed-use residential, was on Tuesday's agenda for final action. Separately, the BelRed code rewrite heard October 28 sets up shared rules for the city's mixed-use districts, Wilburton included. Source

October 15, 2026

Council's final vote on the EverGlen Village rezone is tentatively set for Tuesday, October 20. It would change 0.62 acre at 12500 NE 10th Place from professional office to midrise mixed-use residential zoning, and the Hearing Examiner recommends approval. Source

October 8, 2026

The Grand Connection Crossing over I-405 now has an updated cost: $265.5 million, up from the $175 million to $230 million range on the project page last week. The city plans to start construction in late 2027 or early 2028 and finish by 2031. General contractor Kraemer North America is on board, and the tax-increment district that will help pay for it has been set up. Source

October 1, 2026

The pedestrian bridge that would carry the Grand Connection over I-405 into Wilburton is at 30% design and moving: minimum 30 feet wide, widening to 40 over the freeway, with entry points and elevators at 112th and 116th Avenues NE and separated paths for walking and rolling. Cost is estimated at $175 to $230 million, $46.7 million is committed in the capital plan, and the city's 2026 milestones are 60% design, the tax-increment analysis and selecting the general contractor. Opening is anticipated in 2030. Source

September 24, 2026

The Kelsey Creek Farm Fair is Saturday, October 3, 11 to 4 at 410 130th Place SE. On-site parking is for vendors and disabled visitors only — free parking is at International School, and shuttles run continuously from the Wilburton Park and Ride and Bannerwood Sports Park between 10:45 and 4:15. Admission, parking and many activities are free. Source

September 17, 2026

The Wilburton Trestle rebuild found far more rot than expected: about 1,600 bolts and nearly 2,000 timbers replaced, the budget up to roughly $43.5 million, and the Eastrail opening pushed to mid-2027. Your streets were in the first 20 mph wave. Source · King County

Questions people ask

Is this a real estate newsletter?

No. It is a Bellevue newsletter that happens to know the housing market. Three of the four sections are about living here. The fourth tells you what homes are selling for, because in Bellevue that is news too.

Who writes it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publisher. Every item comes from Bellevue's own public record (council agendas, permit notices, school district and transit updates, parks calendars) and links its source. It is researched and written with AI tools, and an editor reads every issue before it goes out.

How often, and is it really free?

Once a week, Thursday at 6 AM, about five minutes to read. Free, no paywall, unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Who owns it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publishing company. The newsletter is free, and no sponsor buys coverage. Our standards.

What happens with my address?

It is used for one thing: a monthly estimate of what your home would sell for today. It is shared with a local real estate brokerage under the same ownership as our publisher, which may contact you about your home, and is never sold or posted. Remove it any time by replying to any issue.
