The latest in Eastgate

November 12, 2026

A land-use application on 152nd Place SE would remove a dead 35-inch western hemlock from a stream buffer, backed by an arborist report. Comments are open through Nov. 18 at 5 p.m. (file 26-123846-GJ). Source

November 5, 2026

Eastgate Park, at 14500 SE Newport Way, sits on the South Bellevue Community Center campus. It has a ballfield, four pickleball courts, a play area, nature trails and picnic tables. Source

October 29, 2026

Bellevue's draft 2027 state agenda asks the Legislature for $1 million for Eastgate Commons Park. Council took up the draft October 13; the session opens January 11. More on Airfield Park in Reader asked below. Source

October 22, 2026

The city released its call for a design-build team for Bellevue Airfield Park in September and expects to hire one this year. The master plan includes an aquatic facility, pickleball and basketball courts, a flex field, a playground and splash pad. Design starts in 2027; phase one construction is expected in 2030–31. Source

October 15, 2026

A new trail and trailhead will connect Eastgate Park to SE 42nd Street. It is one of the projects residents voted onto the Eastgate–Factoria enhancement budget, it includes a youth job-training component, and it is now expected in summer 2027. Source

October 8, 2026

Two projects are funded in all three budget scenarios. Improvements at the interchange of 150th Avenue SE, SE 37th Street and the eastbound I-90 off-ramp are funded in full ($1.64 million). The Bellevue College Connection gets design money ($2.64 million). The Eastgate neighborhood plan is still scheduled for council on November 10. Source

October 1, 2026

Two dates. The Eastgate neighborhood area plan cleared its Planning Commission hearing on September 23 and goes to City Council on November 10. Separately, the city released a request for qualifications on September 17 for a design-build team on Bellevue Airfield Park — 27.5 acres off I-90 with a community park and an aquatic center in the adopted master plan; design is expected to start in the first half of 2027. Plan · Park

September 24, 2026

The draft Eastgate neighborhood area plan reached its final review: it was on the Planning Commission's agenda Wednesday night, September 23, 6:30 p.m. in Room 1E-113 at City Hall. The plan came out of about ten months of community input and covers neighborhood identity, mixed-use centers, gathering space, mobility and environment. The commission's recommendation goes to council next. Agenda · Plan

September 17, 2026

The dual-use courts at Eastgate Park went pickleball-only on September 1; tennis nets are gone and a resurfacing with pickleball-only lines is planned. Bellevue Big Picture School now occupies the former Eastgate Elementary building. Source

Questions people ask

Is this a real estate newsletter?

No. It is a Bellevue newsletter that happens to know the housing market. Three of the four sections are about living here. The fourth tells you what homes are selling for, because in Bellevue that is news too.

Who writes it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publisher. Every item comes from Bellevue's own public record (council agendas, permit notices, school district and transit updates, parks calendars) and links its source. It is researched and written with AI tools, and an editor reads every issue before it goes out.

How often, and is it really free?

Once a week, Thursday at 6 AM, about five minutes to read. Free, no paywall, unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Who owns it?

Neighborhood Press LLC, an independent publishing company. The newsletter is free, and no sponsor buys coverage. Our standards.

What happens with my address?

It is used for one thing: a monthly estimate of what your home would sell for today. It goes to a local real estate brokerage under the same ownership as our publisher only if you tick the sharing box on the form; that brokerage may then contact you about your home. Leave the box empty and it stays with us. Never sold or posted. Remove it any time by replying to any issue.
